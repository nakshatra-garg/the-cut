Plan reference

Deload

  • Every 6th week: same weights, fewer sets (the app trims them), stop 3–4 reps short of failure
  • Cardio stays easy. Weigh-ins and food logging carry on as normal.

Nutrition

  • ~2,500 kcal/day average, 3 meals
  • Protein 175–190 g, fat 70–80 g, carbs fill the rest
  • Keep roti/rice, control portions. Protein in every meal.
  • No cheat days. If dinner is rich, keep the rest of the day lean and log it honestly.
  • Adjust: 7-day average loss under ~0.3 kg/week for 2–3 weeks, cut ~150–200 kcal. Over ~1 kg/week, add food back.
  • Pace: 105 kg by April is a stretch. 110 kg is the realistic checkpoint.

Training rules

  • Most working sets: 1–3 reps in reserve
  • Preserve strength, don't chase PRs every week
  • Lift ~50–60 min. Cardio is support, not punishment.
  • Hit the top of the rep range with clean form, then add load modestly

Pain rules

  • Foot/ankle: pain-free cardio only. Persistent pain, limping, or pain in daily life means get it assessed.
  • Knee sleeves are fine for comfort, not for masking pain
  • Shoulders: controlled pressing, no forced ranges

Check-in every 2–4 weeks

  • Weight trend down, waist down (47 in at the start), strength mostly held, recovery manageable, no worsening pain

Train hard enough to keep muscle. Recover well enough to keep training.
