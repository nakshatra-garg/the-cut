# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

An installed iPhone web app (PWA) designed to look and behave like a native iOS 26 app. A MacBook is a secondary screen.

## Users

One person: the owner. Personal use, not a product for others. Trains in a commercial gym six days a week and eats an Indian diet (roti/rice, three meals, protein every meal).

## Product Purpose

Get from 127.4 kg to 105 kg without losing muscle. Success means the 7-day average weight keeps falling, strength holds, and most days get closed.

## Positioning

Built around this one person's real plan rather than a generic template: the actual weekly split (Chest + Triceps, Back + Biceps, Legs, Chest + Shoulders + Triceps, Lower + Core, Functional + Conditioning), a 6-week deload cycle, a 158 g+ daily protein target, and coaching that adapts each set and catches up after missed days.

## Operating Context

- Used mostly at the gym: one hand, sweaty, between sets, in no fixed exercise order.
- Morning weigh-in (a 6:30 reminder) and an evening close of the day.
- Data syncs, encrypted when a passphrase is set, to a private GitHub repo; push reminders run from GitHub Actions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Private data: nothing personal leaves the device except the encrypted sync and the numbers sent to the AI coach.
- Exercise demo media is © Gym visual: hot-linked or processed on the device only, never stored in the public repo or committed in derived form.
- AI uses free models only (OpenRouter).
- Occasional foot/ankle niggle, not a chronic injury: don't over-warn about it.
- Follow Apple's Human Interface Guidelines.

## Product Principles

- The 7-day average beats any single weigh-in.
- Honest over flattering.
- Fast at the gym: everything works one-handed, mid-workout, in any order.
- Protect muscle while cutting.
